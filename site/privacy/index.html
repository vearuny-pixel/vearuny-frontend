Vearuny Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Vearuny Kitchen (“Vearuny”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information used in connection with our services.

Information We Collect

We may collect and process information necessary to operate our meal subscription and related services, including customer names, phone numbers, delivery addresses and locations, order information, subscription details, communication information, and other information voluntarily provided to us.

How We Use Information

We use this information to process orders, manage subscriptions, coordinate deliveries, provide customer support, maintain business records, improve our services, and operate internal business automation.

Google API Services

Vearuny Automation may connect to Google services, including Google Sheets and limited Google Drive functionality, through Google OAuth authorization.

Google API access is used only for legitimate Vearuny business operations, including synchronizing registrations, orders, subscription information, and operational records.

We do not sell Google user data, use Google user data for advertising, or provide Google user data to third parties for advertising purposes.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate our services, including hosting, delivery, payment, communication, and business software providers.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for operational, accounting, legal, security, and business requirements.

Google Account Authorization

Users who authorize Google access may revoke that authorization at any time through their Google Account security settings.

Your Privacy Requests

You may contact us to ask questions about your information or request appropriate access, correction, or deletion.
